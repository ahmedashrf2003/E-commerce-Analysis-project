# E-Commerce Performance Analysis

A quick summary of our e-commerce performance analysis, focusing on key marketing insights and simple action steps.

📈 Key Insights :

1) Conversion Rate: Highest in January (17.3%) and lowest in October (6.1%). Sales peak around seasonal items like Ski Boots and Kayaks.

2) Social Media Engagement: Views, clicks, and likes dropped over the year (Views down 48%, Likes down 76%). Blogs brought in the most views compared to video and social posts.

3) Customer Sentiment: Average rating is 3.69 out of 5 (target is 4.0). Most reviews are positive (275 positive vs. 82 negative), but negative feedback highlights areas to improve.

# 💡 Key Recommendations

1) Boost Top Products: Run targeted promotions during peak seasonal months (especially Jan and Sept) for top categories like Kayaks, Ski Boots, and Baseball Gloves.

2) Update Content Strategy: Refresh social media posts and videos with clearer call-to-actions (CTAs) to stop the decline in likes and clicks.

3) Fix Product & Service Issues: Address common complaints in negative reviews to lift the average customer rating past the 4.0 target.
